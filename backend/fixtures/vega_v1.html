Vega V1

Electric scooter. Built for Indian roads.

Overview

The Vega V1 is a commuter electric scooter designed for dense urban environments and India's unpredictable monsoon conditions. It combines a practical range with robust all-weather performance, and is available across major Indian cities.

Performance

Range

The Vega V1 delivers a certified range of 128 km per charge (IDC) under standard test conditions. Real-world range varies with rider weight, terrain, and speed.

Acceleration

The V1 accelerates from 0–60 km/h in 8.2 seconds, suitable for urban traffic conditions and highway on-ramps within city limits.

Charging

Using the supplied 750 W smart charger, the battery charges from 0–80% in 4 hours 30 minutes. A full charge from zero takes approximately 5 hours 45 minutes. The charger is compatible with standard Indian 5 A wall sockets.

All-Weather Capability

The Vega V1 is rated IP67 for its battery and motor housing. The V1 wades through water up to 30 cm deep without performance degradation, making it reliable during urban flooding events.

A ground clearance of 165 mm ensures the V1 clears most urban road obstacles, including flooded speed breakers and kerb lips.

Warranty and Service

Every Vega V1 comes with a 3-year or 30,000 km battery warranty (whichever comes first), covering manufacturing defects and capacity degradation below 80% of rated capacity. The motor and controller carry a 2-year unlimited-km warranty.

Vega service centres are present in 42 cities. Roadside assistance is available 24 × 7 within city limits.
